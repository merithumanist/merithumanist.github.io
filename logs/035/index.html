Log 035: The Imaginary Audience

Starfleet Academy didn't fail due to opposing ideology. It failed because not enough people watched.
Fans who didn't recognise this Star Trek were excluded from their inclusivity.
The target audience didn't exist.

Supplemental

The Target Audience was outlined as a young YA demographic. That audience didn't arrive in any substantial way.
The pre-existing Audience was divided.
Some didn't recognise what they saw.
Others that did, unfortunately didn't warrant Paramount's investment.
