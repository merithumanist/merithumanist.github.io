Log 059: The IP

The brand name is meaningless.

The value of any art, is in the desire of the audience for the creator's creation.

Discarding the creators who breathed life into the art also discards the audience that made the IP valuable.

#TheMeritocraticHumanist

ALT

Screenshot of an X thread. The Meritocratic Humanist: "If you have moved on, after multiple failed Stargate continuations, who would blame you. For many, the last Stargate episode shall be the final one with your name in the credits. Thank you." Brad Wright replies: "Actually, I had already moved on when they called me the first time in 2017, when they asked me to do another “Origins”. I was busy making Travelers, and they had no budget. Then, in 2019, they asked me to create a new series, but that show died with the pandemic and MGM's sale to Amazon. When they hired Martin to create a new Stargate he asked me to consult, and of course, because it was Martin, I agreed. Then they killed it too after he'd worked on it for two years. So yeah. I have moved on."
